# 5-optimizer: most useful findings
1. Almost every optimizer variable and flag acts per statement plan. The tuner collects digests but not plans. The largest missing input is EXPLAIN FORMAT=TREE / EXPLAIN ANALYZE (MariaDB: ANALYZE FORMAT=JSON) of the top digests by SUM_TIMER_WAIT. Without it the tuner cannot tell whether a flag's strategy is used at all.
2. Test flags per digest first with SET_VAR / optimizer hints (NO_INDEX_MERGE, SEMIJOIN(...), NO_ICP, JOIN_ORDER...). This measures the gain on the statement before any global change, and the bound is that digest's share of SUM_TIMER_WAIT.
3. High-impact but workload-specific: hypergraph_optimizer (9.7 community, default off), subquery_to_derived (Oracle documents frequent regressions; per-digest only), optimizer_search_depth (planning time on joins of 10+ tables). None of these matter for sysbench/TPC-C-style OLTP.
4. optimizer_search_depth, optimizer_prune_level and eq_range_index_dive_limit need planning-time evidence: performance_schema stage/sql/statistics and stage/sql/optimizing time per digest. Stage instruments are off by default and not collected yet.
5. range_optimizer_max_mem_size has a precise signal that is not collected: warning ER_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED (3170) in events_errors_summary_global_by_error (or digest SUM_WARNINGS) together with full scans.
6. MRR / BKA (and MariaDB join_cache_level 5-8): the right evidence is the off-CPU data-page-read wait share during secondary-index range or join digests, plus Handler_mrr_init. Both are collected. These settings help only on disk-bound data and add CPU when data is in memory.
7. index_condition_pushdown: INNODB_METRICS module_icp (icp_attempts/icp_match/icp_no_match/icp_out_of_range, disabled by default; MariaDB status Handler_icp_*) shows how many row lookups ICP saves. Not collected. Turning ICP off is almost never a gain.
8. index_merge_intersection and prefer_ordering_index are the usual flags worth turning off. The signals are a high rows-examined/rows-sent ratio in the digest (collected) plus 'Using intersect' or an ordered-index LIMIT scan in the plan (not collected).
9. MariaDB statistics (use_stat_tables, histogram_size, optimizer_use_condition_selectivity) need mysql.column_stats contents and r_filtered vs filtered from ANALYZE FORMAT=JSON (not collected). MariaDB performance_schema is off by default, so the digests may be missing too.
10. No-ops or none expected: hash_join (8.0.19+), engine_condition_pushdown (NDB only), max_length_for_sort_data (MySQL 8.0.20+), use_index_extensions, use_invisible_indexes. innodb_stats_on_metadata only matters with non-persistent stats.
