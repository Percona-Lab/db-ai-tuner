# 2-io-flushing-redo: most useful findings

1. Redo capacity (innodb_redo_log_capacity / innodb_log_file_size) is the top lever here. The decisive signal is which mechanism drives flushing: innodb_metrics `buffer_flush_n_to_flush_by_age` vs `buffer_flush_n_to_flush_by_dirty_page`, plus checkpoint age vs `log_max_modified_age_async/_sync`. The tuner collects checkpoint age but none of these. Add them.
2. Sync-flush stalls are not collected. Add `buffer_flush_sync_total_pages`, `buffer_flush_sync_waits` and `log_on_file_space_waits`. They give the measured impact bound for redo size, io_capacity(_max), adaptive flushing and flush_sync.
3. For io_capacity in either direction, add `buffer_flush_n_to_flush_requested` vs `buffer_flush_avg_page_rate` (is flushing clipped at the cap?). Pair it with device util/read await, which the tuner already has. Raising io_capacity on a saturated disk measured -12..-35%.
4. Write amplification needs a per-trx derived metric: Innodb_buffer_pool_pages_flushed per trx next to redo KiB per trx. It is the main evidence for raising max_dirty_pages_pct_lwm, adaptive_flushing_lwm and redo capacity.
5. Free-page waits: Innodb_buffer_pool_wait_free is collected. Add `buffer_LRU_get_free_waits`, `buffer_LRU_single_flush_num_scan` and `buffer_LRU_batch_scanned` (MySQL/Percona), or `Innodb_buffer_pool_pages_LRU_flushed`/`_LRU_freed` (MariaDB). They show whether lru_scan_depth, lru_flush_size, Percona lru_threads or empty_free_list_algorithm can help.
6. Change buffering (high impact on ingest) needs `ibuf_merges*`/`ibuf_size` (MySQL innodb_metrics) or `Innodb_ibuf_*` (MariaDB status), plus off-CPU page-read time attributed to INSERT/UPDATE digests. It does not exist in MariaDB 11.x.
7. Commit-path redo variables (log_writer_threads, the three log_spin variables, MariaDB log_file_write_through/buffering) need the innodb_metrics log module counters `log_on_flush_waits/_wait_loops/_no_waits` and `log_flush_avg_time`. They also need per-thread CPU of log_writer/log_flusher/page_cleaner threads (performance_schema.threads THREAD_OS_ID + /proc). Neither is collected.
8. Doublewrite placement (+6% measured) can be bounded from Innodb_dblwr_pages_written / pages flushed and the per-device split of the dblwr file I/O time. Add Innodb_dblwr_pages_written and the `dblwr_flush_wait_events` metric (dblwr module is off by default). On MySQL 8.0, pages per dblwr write at the limit of 4 points to raising innodb_doublewrite_pages.
9. fsync-related variables (use_fdatasync, data/log write_through, O_DIRECT_NO_FSYNC) need per-file-type sync latency: performance_schema file_summary_by_event_name SUM_TIMER_MISC. The tuner collects counts only. Device FUA support (/sys/block/*/queue/fua) and rotational flag decide whether these and flush_neighbors can matter.
10. No measurable signal in steady benchmarks: innodb_fsync_threshold, innodb_idle_flush_pct, innodb_autoextend_increment, innodb_log_files_in_group, innodb_flushing_avg_loops (bursty loads only). innodb_parallel_doublewrite_path is ignored after PS 8.0.22. rocksdb_max_background_jobs needs Rocksdb_stall_* counters when MyRocks is used.
